LossProof

A room-by-room record of what you own — photos, receipts, serial numbers and what each thing would cost to replace — so that a claim starts from evidence instead of memory.

Part of Proof-Apps — a series of apps whose job is to hold up as evidence.

  • Items with their photos, receipts, serial numbers, rooms, categories and prices
  • Your insurance policy, and the incidents attached to it
  • Reminders when a warranty runs out or a policy comes up for renewal
  • A PDF report for the insurer, or a plain ZIP, PDF or CSV export
  • Password-protected backup, encrypted with AES-256; the key lives in the Android Keystore
  • Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock
  • Receipt text recognition runs on the device
  • No internet permission at all — the app cannot send anything anywhere
  • Free for 15 items; one purchase lifts the limit, with no subscription

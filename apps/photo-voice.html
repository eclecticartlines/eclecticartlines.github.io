Photo Voice: PDF, Image Reader

Photograph a page or open a PDF and listen to it. Built for the hard cases other readers refuse: scanned books, photocopies, out-of-print editions.

  • Columns and two-page spreads read in the right order
  • Hyphens rejoined; page numbers, running heads and front matter dropped — you hear the book, not the ISBN
  • An honest verdict on every photo — good, fair or poor — and how to retake it
  • Formulas are announced as “formula here” instead of read out as a jumble of digits
  • Your own pronunciation list for words the voice keeps mangling
  • Works with TalkBack, for people who listen rather than read
  • Recognition runs on the phone, offline, in 80+ languages
  • First 40 minutes free, then a single purchase — no ads, no subscription

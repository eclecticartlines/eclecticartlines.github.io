HouseProof

Proof about the home itself — its condition, what was repaired and when, kept with photos and dates.

Part of Proof-Apps — a series of apps whose job is to hold up as evidence.

  • A record of the dwelling and the work done in it: by whom, for how much, when, with photos before and after
  • Built for owners, not for deposit disputes
  • Everything stays on the phone — no account, no cloud, no internet permission at all

Smart Coloring Book: Postcards

166 hand-drawn pages in 8 collections. Smart coloring finds the tiny details and fills them in one tap — then the finished page becomes a postcard.

  • A pipette picks any colour straight out of the picture

  • Collections from mandalas and flowers to steampunk animals
  • Works offline, no ads, 11 languages

Art Jigsaw: Photo Puzzle Maker

Turn any photo from your gallery into a jigsaw puzzle, or take apart one of 340+ original pictures — around sixty of them paintings of mine.

  • 5×5 to 9×9, and the pieces are cut differently every time
  • Collections from palette-knife still lifes to steam-age robots
  • Pieces click into place, join into groups and move as one
  • A piece lost on the board wiggles and glows until you find it
  • Works offline, no ads at all, 19 languages

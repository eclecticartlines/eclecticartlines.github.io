Altın Takip Defteri

A private notebook for the gold you keep at home: grams, carat, where each piece came from and what it cost — and what it would fetch today.

  • Value shown in your own yardstick — months of rent, or progress toward a goal
  • 8 to 24 carat, with stones left out of the weight
  • Sold, gifted or lost pieces move to an archive instead of vanishing
  • Fingerprint, PIN or pattern lock, and one tap hides every figure
  • Encrypted on the phone; password-protected backup and PDF export
  • Prices from the Altınkaynak free market, refreshed twice a day, with the last known price shown offline
  • No charts, no profit tracking — it is a notebook, not an investment tool
  • Five items free, then a single purchase

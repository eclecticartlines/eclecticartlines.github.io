TotalProof

Collect everything on one topic — photos, screenshots, voice, documents, notes and who said what — and take it out as one clear text for an AI chat, or as a PDF. Collected, not concluded: it keeps your materials and assumptions, it gives no advice.

  • A batch for each topic: photos, voice, documents, notes, people and facts
  • One dump with a ready prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek — or a PDF
  • No internet access, no account, no built-in AI — nothing leaves the phone unless you send it

Privacy Policy

Palette: Color from Photo  ·  Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Eclectic Art Lines ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Palette: Color from Photo mobile application ("the app").

Your Photos Stay on Your Device

The app extracts a color palette from a photo you take with the camera or choose from your gallery. All processing happens on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app keeps the following only in its private storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Saved palettes (History) — colors, date and a small preview copy of the photo, only if you save a palette.
  • Favorites — colors and palettes you mark with ♡.
  • Settings — theme (light / dark), language, which tips you have already seen, and the last settings of the mockup and Material 3 theme screens for each palette.
  • Purchase status — whether you have bought “Remove ads” and/or “Pro”.

You can delete any saved palette or favorite in the app at any time; uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

Sharing and Export

When you export a palette (for example PNG, ASE, Procreate swatches or theme code) or send a mockup as an image, the app creates the file on your device and opens the Android share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to take a photo when you tap "Camera".
  • Photos / gallery — only to open the photo you pick.
  • Internet — only to load advertisements and to handle purchases through Google Play.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone or files beyond the photo you pick.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect, store or receive your name, email address, photos, palettes or location. We do not use our own analytics.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Advertising ID;
  • IP address and the general region derived from it;
  • information about ad views and interactions, and basic device information.

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Ad settings.

You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

Purchases

The app offers two optional one-time in-app purchases: “Remove ads” and “Pro” (professional tools; Pro also removes ads). There is no subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive your payment details. The app only stores on your device the fact that a purchase was made, and Google Play confirms it when you restore purchases.

Third-Party Content

Color libraries in the app are used under their open licenses: Tailwind CSS colors (MIT, © Tailwind Labs, Inc.), Open Color (MIT, © heeyeun), Nord (MIT, © Sven Greb) and Material Design colors (Apache 2.0, © Google). The names belong to their owners; this app is not affiliated with them. The full license texts are in the app: Settings → Licenses.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Privacy Policy

Eclectic Puzzle  ·  Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Eclectic Art Lines ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Eclectic Puzzle mobile application.

Information We Do Not Collect

Eclectic Puzzle does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not require account registration, and we do not have access to your name, email address, or location.

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain data to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Usage data and interaction with ads

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings (Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation).

Analytics

The app may use Google Play's built-in analytics (Firebase/Play Console) to understand general usage patterns such as session length and crash reports. This data is aggregated and not linked to individual users.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: eclecticartlines@gmail.com

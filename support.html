Common questions

My progress disappeared

My games have no accounts, so your progress is stored only on your phone. It is lost if you clear the app's data, reinstall the app or move to a new phone. If it disappeared without any of that, please write to me — it's a bug and I want to fix it.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep the games free for everyone. They are shown by Google AdMob. You can turn off personalised ads in your phone settings: Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads Personalisation on older Android versions).

Photo Voice: PDF, Image Reader: I paid, but reading is still limited

The purchase is made through Google Play and is tied to your Google account. Make sure you're signed in to Play with the same account you used to buy, then tap Restore purchases in the app. If it still doesn't work, write to me and include your order number from the Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.).

Photo Voice: PDF, Image Reader: a word is read out strangely

Some words are ambiguous in writing — abbreviations, numbers with units, and words that are spelled the same but stressed differently. I keep adding these to the app's pronunciation dictionaries as they turn up. If you hear something wrong, write to me with the sentence exactly as it appeared on the page, and I'll fix that case in the next update.

412 Sesli Masal İnternetsiz: a story won't download

The stories are streamed and downloaded from my server, so the first play of each one needs an internet connection. Once a story is downloaded it plays offline. If a download stops halfway, open the story again — it resumes rather than starting over. If it keeps failing on Wi-Fi, try mobile data, and tell me which story it was.

Altın Takip Defteri: I'm changing phones — how do I move my box?

Make a backup before you give up the old phone. The app is deliberately excluded from Android's cloud backup, so nothing moves across on its own — your own backup file is the only way. Save it somewhere you'll still have access to afterwards, and remember the password you set.

Altın Takip Defteri: I forgot my backup password

Then that backup can't be opened — not by you and not by me. The file is encrypted with your password alone, and I hold no copy of it and no master key. This is the price of the app keeping no servers and no accounts. If the old phone still works, open the app there and make a fresh backup with a password you'll remember.

Altın Takip Defteri: the gold price looks wrong

Prices come from Altınkaynak's open-market data and are updated periodically, so they can lag the live market, and the buy/sell spread at a jeweller will differ again. Treat the figure as a reference for your own records, not as a quote.

How do I delete my data?

I don't keep any of your data on servers — everything stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

I have an idea for a game or a picture

I'd love to hear it! Many improvements in my games started as a message from a player.

Photo Voice: PDF, Image Reader

On Google Play

Photograph a page or open a PDF, EPUB or FB2 and listen to it. Built for the hard cases other readers refuse: scanned books, photocopies, out-of-print editions.

  • Columns and two-page spreads read in the right order
  • Hyphens rejoined; page numbers, running heads and front matter dropped
  • An honest verdict on every photo — good, fair or poor — and how to retake it
  • Your own pronunciation list for words the voice keeps mangling
  • Works with TalkBack, for listening rather than reading
  • Recognition runs on the phone, offline, in 80+ languages
  • First 40 minutes free, then a single purchase — no ads

About

Building the kind of apps people assume require an entire team

Automation, privacy and real-world data — the three things that usually take a department each. Here they are one person’s work: the idea, the design, the code, the testing, the release, and the store listing written out in 24 languages.

I come from graphic design and pre-press production, so I care a lot about how things look and feel: calm colors, clean screens, nothing that shouts at you. And because nothing leaves your phone, privacy isn’t a policy page — it’s how the apps are built.

  • Games built with Unity, apps with Flutter
  • Apps and store listings in 24 languages, written by hand
  • No accounts or sign-ups in any of my apps
  • Each app links to the others — if you like one, try the next

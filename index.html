About

One developer, full cycle.

Eclectic Art Lines is me — an independent mobile developer based in Istanbul. Every game and app here is made by one person from start to finish: the idea, the design, the code, the testing and the release.

I come from graphic design and pre-press production, so I care a lot about how things look and feel: calm colors, clean screens, nothing that shouts at you.

  • Games built with Unity, apps with Flutter
  • No accounts or sign-ups in any of my apps
  • Each game links to the others — if you like one, try the next
